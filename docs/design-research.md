# BOECL publishing design research — 2026-08-08

Reviewed Onedio, Webrazzi, Aposto, Medyascope, The Verge, and news information-architecture guidance. Onedio demonstrates high-volume visual category discovery; Webrazzi uses specialist authority, author/time signals, reports, and events; Aposto deliberately reduces noise around curated analysis; Medyascope makes reader support part of its trust model. The Verge's feed demonstrates fast editor-curated discovery, but its visual intensity should not be copied wholesale.

BOECL direction: a calm editorial canvas, clear lead hierarchy, compact discovery cards, visible source/author/commercial signals, restrained advertising, and secondary locale/theme controls. Mobile collapses hierarchy instead of shrinking desktop columns. Navigation stays link-based. Light, dark, and OS modes use accessible contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and locale-safe fluid type.
